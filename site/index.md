# Song2Vid

Turn one song and one image into a video. Drop them in, click once, and get an MP4.

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/devin-thomas/song2vid-app/releases/latest/download/Song2Vid-1.1.0.zip)

Free · Version 1.1.0 · Requires macOS 14 or later

## Screenshots

Song2Vid 1.1.0 on a Mac.

Choose a song and an image, pick a frame shape and resolution, and the preview shows the video's frame.

9:16 for vertical video, with the whole image kept. 1:1 for a square post. Drag the preview to choose the crop. Make Video saves an MP4 and offers to show it in Finder.

### The song2vid command

Make a video from the terminal with `song2vid make`. See the [command docs](https://song2vid.pages.dev/docs/). A frame from the video it made.

## Or ask your agent to do it

`Use Song2Vid to make a video from my song and image. Instructions: https://song2vid.pages.dev/agent.md`

## How to get started

1. Unzip the download and move Song2Vid to your Applications folder.
2. Open it. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Choose a song and an image, then click **Make Video**.
