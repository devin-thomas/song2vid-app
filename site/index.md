# Song2Vid

Turn one song and one image into a video. Drop them in, click once, and get an MP4.

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/devin-thomas/song2vid-app/releases/latest/download/Song2Vid-1.2.0.zip)

Requires macOS 14 or later

[Download for Windows](https://github.com/devin-thomas/song2vid-app/releases/latest/download/Song2Vid-1.2.0-windows-x64.zip)

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Free · Version 1.2.0

## How to get started

### On a Mac

1. Unzip the download and move Song2Vid to your Applications folder.
2. Open it. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Choose a song and an image, then click **Make Video**.

### On Windows

1. Unzip the download and run **Install Song2Vid.cmd**. It installs Song2Vid for your account, adds it to the Start menu and needs no administrator rights.
2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open Song2Vid from the Start menu, choose a song and an image, then click **Make Video**.

Song2Vid makes H.264 videos, and HEVC videos on computers that can make them.
