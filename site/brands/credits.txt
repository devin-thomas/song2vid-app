Song2Vid uses product artwork only to identify the agents named on the download page. Trademarks remain with their owners; inclusion does not imply endorsement. The files are copies of their sources with geometry and fills preserved. The only edit: in chatgpt-original.svg and claude-original.svg a space was added where two path numbers ran together (for example 1.5.5 became 1.5 .5), so the repo's address scan does not mistake them for addresses; the numbers, and the rendered pixels, are identical. The page recolours the monochrome marks (ChatGPT, Grok Bot) to white in dark mode with a CSS filter; no colours are added.
